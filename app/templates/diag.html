🔍 Noise scan

off

Arms edge detection on every free GPIO pin at once so you can find where a signal actually arrives: start the scan, insert a coin, and the pin carrying it jumps to the top. Unconnected pins float and pick up noise — judge by the counts, since a real coin gives a clean burst on one pin. Stop it when done; it costs CPU, and manual pin testing is disabled while it runs. IRQ pins (PA*) catch every edge in hardware; poll pins (PC*/PD14) are sampled ~100×/s and can miss pulses under ~10 ms, which is why the coin acceptor may only be assigned to an IRQ pin.

Restart the software

Restarts the portal service only. Takes a few seconds and the board stays up. Try this first — it clears almost anything a reboot would, without the wait.

Reboot the board

Full restart of the Orange Pi. Takes about a minute, and you will not be able to reach this page until it is back. Coin acceptor, firewall and shaping are all rebuilt on boot.
