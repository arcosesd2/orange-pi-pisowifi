🔍 Noise scan — listen on ALL pins at once

off

Arms edge detection on every free GPIO pin simultaneously and records which pins produce activity. Use it to find where a signal actually arrives: start the scan, insert a coin (or press the button / wiggle the wire), and the pin carrying it jumps to the top of the table. Note: unconnected pins float and can pick up random electrical noise — judge by the counts, a real coin gives a clean burst on one pin. Stop the scan when done (it uses CPU), the results stay until the next scan. Manual pin testing above is disabled while scanning.
IRQ pins (PA*) catch every edge via hardware interrupt; poll pins (PC*/PD14 — no interrupt line on the H3) are sampled ~100×/s, so pulses shorter than ~10 ms may be missed there. The coin acceptor must sit on an IRQ pin — the hardware form only lets you pick those.
