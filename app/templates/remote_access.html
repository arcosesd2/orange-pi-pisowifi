Offsite backup

A backup stored on this machine's own SD card protects against almost nothing — the card is the single most likely thing to fail, and it takes both copies with it. Name another machine on your tailnet and the scheduled backup_offsite job sends a copy there.

Sent with Taildrop, so there are no SSH keys to distribute. Collect them on that machine with tailscale file get — on Windows the Tailscale app saves them for you. Offsite copies exclude secrets: the admin hash and remote key stay on the machine, because this copy leaves it.

Then add a backup_offsite job under Schedule — nightly, at a quiet hour.

Connection sharing

A customer who turns on their phone's hotspot can serve several people from one payment. This detects it by watching the TTL of arriving packets: a device that forwards for others shows two different TTLs at once — its own traffic and the traffic it is relaying.
