Portal sounds

Plays on the customer's phone, not on the machine — the board has no speaker. Sound is a bonus on top of the on-screen numbers: phones block audio until the customer taps something, and the cut-down browser some phones open a captive portal in may not play it at all.

Leaving the uploads empty uses short built-in tones that are generated in the browser and cost nothing to send. Upload your own only if you want them — every customer downloads the file over the link they are sharing, on every visit. Keep them under 256 KB; anything longer than about a second is an annoyance, not a feature.
